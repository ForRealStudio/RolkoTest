Our expertise

Services

From digital capture to physical parts, we develop practical solutions for real engineering challenges.

01. 3D Scanning

3D scanning captures the geometry of an existing component and creates a digital representation. It can support inspection, redesign and the recreation of parts.

02. Technical 3D Printing

3D printing enables the production of custom parts and prototypes using suitable technical materials. Material and design choices depend on the intended application and operating conditions.

03. Reverse Engineering

When an original component is damaged or difficult to obtain, reverse engineering can help recreate its geometry from an existing part or available technical information.

04. Mechanical Engineering

Engineering work focuses on understanding the application and developing a solution that fits the specific technical requirements.
